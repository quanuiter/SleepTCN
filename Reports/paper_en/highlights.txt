Highlights

- ResNet-1D-TCN forward passes were 3.76-fold faster than a CNN-BiLSTM control.
- Its cross-cohort gain was confirmed on 500 held-out SHHS1 participants.
- Band-pass filtering accounted for most of the cross-cohort gain.
- Gains over the baseline exceeded the spread across five training seeds.
- All zero-shot pipelines scored most N3 (deep-sleep) epochs as N2.
