Highlights

- A single-channel ZleepAnlystNet baseline was reproduced within 0.007 macro-F1.
- A ResNet-1D-TCN pipeline matched its accuracy and trained about ten times faster.
- Its SHHS transfer gain was confirmed on 500 held-out participants.
- On a second cohort the baseline scored higher: transfer gains depend on the target.
- Missed deep sleep after transfer went unflagged by model uncertainty.
